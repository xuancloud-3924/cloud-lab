DAY 9 - SERVICE INVESTIGATION

1. What is a service?

A service is a background program that provides a system or application function.

2. What is systemctl?

systemctl is used to manage systemd services.

3. Commands learned:

systemctl status
systemctl start
systemctl stop
systemctl restart
systemctl enable
systemctl disable
systemctl is-active
systemctl is-enabled

4. SSH investigation:

systemctl status ssh
systemctl is-active ssh
systemctl is-enabled ssh

5. Security questions:

- What services are running?
- Is the service expected?
- Who installed the service?
- What does the service do?
- Does the service provide network access?
- Is the service required?

6. Security mindset:

Unexpected services should be investigated before deciding whether they are malicious.
